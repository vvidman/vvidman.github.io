Scaffold Protocol · Part 1

The AI executes. You decide.

A human orchestrator who decides what happens next, and an AI transformation engine that executes exactly one task at a time.

Human orchestrator
Decides what happens next.
AI step
Given a defined input, produces a defined output in a specific format.
Validator
Finds the certainly wrong outputs.
Human decision
Passed output might be good. The human decides.
Retry
Sends the same request again and hopes for a better output
Gives the model no information about what went wrong
Error-driven refinement
The specific violation is mapped to a targeted error message
The message is injected into a new prompt that says exactly what to fix
Run 5: accepted after one automatic refinement. “I'd do it differently, but I can't fault it.”
