Governing AI Coding Assistants · Part 2

Choosing models without an eval lab

Capability is one column of five.

Capability
Does it handle this class of work well?
Cost
Does its price fit the tier?
Latency
Is it fast enough for how this tier is used?
Context
Can it hold the code and history the work needs?
Lifecycle risk
How likely is it to change or disappear soon?
Benchmarks are a filter, not a verdict.
