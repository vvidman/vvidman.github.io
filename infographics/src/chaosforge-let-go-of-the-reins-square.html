ChaosForge · Part 1

Let go of the reins

What if the goal isn't to control every step — but to trust the process?

Scaffold Protocol
A tightly controlled single-agent pipeline
The human reviewed output at each checkpoint
Refinement loops on outputs that were fine
ChaosForge
A multi-agent team working in parallel
Human review gates at the right moments, not every moment
At each gate: accept, edit and accept, or reject
Business Analyst
Reads use cases, produces User Requirements Specifications.
Architect
Turns them into Software Requirements and implementable tasks.
Scrum Master
Builds the sprint plan.
Parallel team
Developers, testers, reviewers and technical writers.
The butterfly effect isn't a risk to be managed. It's the design.
