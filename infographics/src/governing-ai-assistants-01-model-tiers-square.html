Governing AI Coding Assistants · Part 1

Not everyone needs the frontier model

A tier is a class of work, not a model.

Frontier
Multi-step changes, architecture-level reasoning, unfamiliar code.
Matters most
Capability
Workhorse
Daily development: features, fixes, tests, reviews.
Matters most
Balance of quality, speed and cost
Fast iterative
Small, repetitive steps: renames, formatting, boilerplate, quick questions.
Matters most
Latency and cost
Specialised
A workflow built around one specific model's behaviour.
Matters most
Stability of that one model
One default per tier, at most two alternatives.
