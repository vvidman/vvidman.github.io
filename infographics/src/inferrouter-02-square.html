InferRouter · Part 2

Inference strategies

The strategy returns an ordered list of candidates. The execution loop does everything else.

Strategy
Returns an ordered list of candidate providers.
Execution loop
Executes attempts, handles errors, logs, tracks rate limits.
LlamaSharp
Local GGUF model, appended last as the guaranteed fallback.
ChainOfResponsibility
Providers are tried in config order. The most predictable and debuggable option.
WeightedRoundRobin
Traffic is spread in proportion to each provider's DailyRequestLimit.
LeastUsed
Picks the lowest DailyCount / DailyLimit ratio. Ties are random.
The strategy never sees errors or results.
